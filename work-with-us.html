Training & Consulting

Work with us to build lasting intergenerational impact.

From a one-off introductory workshop to an organisation-wide strategy, we guide you through designing, developing, and implementing joyful intergenerational programs.

How we collaborate

Tailored support for your team

Webinars & E-Learning

Accessible, self-paced and live online learning modules introducing staff to the evidence base, safety considerations, and practical magic of intergenerational connection.

Interactive Workshops

Half-day or full-day hands-on workshops for aged care lifestyle teams, early childhood educators, and council staff. Participants leave with ready-to-run session plans and facilitation confidence.

Customised Training

Bespoke professional development tailored to your residential care setting, kindergarten network, or community hub—including joint training sessions that bring partner organisations together before launch.

End-to-End Program Consulting

Comprehensive strategic guidance covering feasibility, local partner matching, risk assessment, pilot evaluation, and long-term sustainability planning.
