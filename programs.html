What we do

Connecting generations where we live, learn, and thrive.

Explore our three core pillars: community play sessions in aged care homes, practitioner training workshops, and tailored organisational consulting.

01 — Programs

Intergenerational Programs

Our signature intergenerational play sessions and story-time take place directly inside aged care homes, bringing together older residents, young children (aged 0–5), and their parents or caregivers in a warm, welcoming environment.

What a session looks like

  • Singing & Music: Familiar songs, rhythm instruments, and musical games that bridge generations effortlessly.
  • Gentle Movement: Inclusive seated and standing movement activities suited to all mobility levels.
  • Interactive Story-Time: Shared picture books that spark conversation, memory-sharing, and imagination.
  • Craft & Group Play: Hands-on creative projects and tactile games designed for pairs of older and younger friends.

02 — Training

Empowering Workshops

We deliver evidence-informed training for individuals and teams across aged care, early childhood education, community services, and local government. Our workshops equip practitioners with practical tools to run safe, engaging, and sustainable programs.

Key workshop topics

  • Foundations of Intergenerational Practice: principles, research, and mutual benefit.
  • Designing Age-Inclusive Activities for residents (including people living with dementia) and early learners.
  • Facilitation Skills: building confidence, managing group dynamics, and fostering spontaneous connection.
  • Risk Management, Safeguarding, and Evaluation frameworks.

03 — Consulting

Purposeful Consulting

Starting a new intergenerational program—or scaling an existing pilot—requires thoughtful planning and strong community partnerships. Our consulting service walks alongside your organisation at every stage.

How we support your organisation

  • Readiness & Partner Matching: Connecting aged care providers with local kindergartens, playgroups, or schools.
  • Co-Design & Curriculum Development: Tailoring program models to your spaces, staffing, and community goals.
  • Implementation Coaching: On-site mentoring and troubleshooting during your launch phase.
  • Impact Measurement: Tools to capture participant wellbeing outcomes and stories of change.
