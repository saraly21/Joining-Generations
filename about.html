About Us

Building friendship and belonging across the ages.

We are a Melbourne-based social enterprise bringing younger and older generations together through play, music, art, stories, and purposeful community practice.

Our Mission

Recreating natural intergenerational communities

In a society that is increasingly age-segregated and where loneliness has become an epidemic, we believe that intergenerational relationships offer a powerful antidote. Historically, people of all ages lived, learned, and worked side by side—sharing wisdom, curiosity, care, and joy every day.

Joining Generations exists to rebuild those bridges. By partnering with aged care homes, early learning services, local councils, and community organisations across Melbourne and beyond, we design and facilitate meaningful shared experiences that enrich the lives of both older adults and young children.

Our Values

What guides our practice

01

Mutual Benefit

Every session is intentionally designed so both older residents and young children feel valued, heard, and engaged as active participants.

02

Joy & Playfulness

Connection flourishes through shared laughter, music, gentle movement, stories, and creative exploration—without pressure or expectation.

03

Dignity & Inclusion

We honour the lived experience of every elder and the wonder of every child, actively challenging ageism in all its forms.

Our Impact

Why it matters

Research and lived experience consistently show that ongoing intergenerational programs yield transformative outcomes:

  • Reduced feelings of isolation and loneliness among aged care residents, accompanied by increased vitality and sense of purpose.
  • Enhanced social-emotional development, empathy, and communication skills in young children aged 0–5.
  • Stronger community networks for families and caregivers who participate alongside their children.
  • Renewed workplace joy and connection for aged care and early childhood staff.
