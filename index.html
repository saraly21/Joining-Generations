Why we’re here

Intergenerational Relationships: A Powerful Antidote to Loneliness

In a society that is increasingly age-segregated and where loneliness has become an epidemic, we believe that intergenerational relationships offer a powerful antidote. They recreate the natural intergenerational communities we used to live and thrive in.

What we do

Connecting generations where we live, learn, and thrive.

The power of bringing older and younger people together to learn from one another, share experiences and create a sense of connection and belonging.

01

Intergenerational Programs

Intergenerational play sessions and story-time in aged care homes, bringing together residents, young children (aged 0–5), and their caregivers. Sessions feature singing and music, gentle movement, story time, group games, and craft activities.
